## What UST Is

UST proposes that the universe is built from a single continuous substrate.

- Fields are behaviors of that substrate  
- Particles are stable vibration patterns  
- Forces are gradients  
- Gravity is deformation  
- Quantum behavior comes from oscillation and update rules  

UST is not an interpretation.  
It is a physical mechanism.
